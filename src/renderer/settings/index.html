Settings

Keyboard shortcuts

Click a field and press a key combination. Backspace clears it, Esc cancels.

After capture

Capturing

Turn off to keep animations and videos playing; the capture is taken when you finish selecting.

Self-timer

Start the timer, then open the menu or hover the tooltip you want to capture.

Saving

Example:
Use {date} {time} {year} {month} {day} {hour} {minute} {second} {app} {title} {mode} {width} {height} {n}. A “/” makes folders, e.g. {year}-{month}/{app} {time}.

Screen recording

GIFs are recorded at up to 15 fps and 960 pixels wide to keep them small. Recordings are saved to the save folder.

Sharing

Register an app at api.imgur.com/oauth2/addclient (choose “anonymous usage”) to get a Client ID. Uploads are public to anyone with the link.
